About SCveris Group

SCveris Group AG is a publicly listed, EMEA-headquartered diversified life sciences and regulated manufacturing conglomerate, incorporated under Swiss law and domiciled in Zurich. Founded in 1987 as a contract pharmaceutical manufacturer, the Group operates across four regulated industries: pharmaceutical & biotech (including a dedicated biopharma arm covering biologics, cell & gene therapy, vaccines, plasma, and tissue products), medical devices, in vitro diagnostics, and food & beverage.

With a unified commitment to quality, compliance, and operational excellence, SCveris Group has selected MasterControl as its enterprise platform for Manufacturing Excellence (Mx / EBR), Quality Excellence (Qx / QMS), and Asset Excellence (Ax / CMMS) across all sites and divisions.
